How the real test works

The SAT is digital now and you take it on a laptop or tablet in the Bluebook app. Two sections:

  • Reading and Writing: 54 questions, 64 minutes, split into two modules. Every question has its own short passage. Nothing longer than about 150 words.
  • Math: 44 questions, 70 minutes, two modules. A built-in Desmos graphing calculator is available the whole time.

It is adaptive: how you do on Module 1 decides whether Module 2 is the easier or harder version. Wrong answers cost you nothing extra, so never leave a question blank. Score range is 400 to 1600.

With time and a half approved: Reading and Writing becomes 96 minutes and Math becomes 105 minutes. The extra time is built into the Bluebook app automatically on test day. Check with your SSD coordinator about how breaks and pacing work in a small-group room, since those details are set by the test center.
